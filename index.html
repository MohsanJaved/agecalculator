Frequently Asked Questions

How is exact age calculated?

Age is calculated by subtracting the year, month, and day of birth from the current date, adjusting for variable month lengths (28, 29, 30, or 31 days) and leap years.

Are my birth dates stored on a server?

No. All calculations are executed locally in your web browser. No personal birth information is transmitted or stored.
